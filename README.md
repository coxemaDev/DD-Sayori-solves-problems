# Doki Doki Sayori solves problems!
<img width="256" height="256" alt="window_icon" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ea5fd7d1-c12b-4962-b1a0-f32d15cc6430" />

Doki Doki: Sayori Solves Problems is a short fan mod announced by independent developers (coxemaDev and julesDev). Unlike the original Sayori, who suffered from deep depression and hid her problems, in this mod she takes on the role of "chief problem solver."
